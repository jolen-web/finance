Getting Started Workflow

Follow these steps to set up your Finance Tracker and start tracking your finances:

Create Your First Account

Start by creating accounts to track your money across different sources.

  • Checking Account: For regular spending and deposits
  • Savings Account: For money you want to save
  • Credit Card: To track credit card balances
  • Cash: For physical cash you carry
  • Digital Wallet: For mobile payment apps
Create Account
Add Your First Transaction

Record income and expenses to track your spending patterns.

  • Use the Quick Add button for fast entry
  • Choose a category for automatic analysis
  • Add notes for additional context
  • Upload receipts with photos
Add Transaction
Customize Your Settings

Personalize your experience by configuring your preferences.

  • Currency: Choose your display currency
  • Dashboard: Select which sections to display
  • Categories: Create custom income and expense categories
  • Theme: Switch between light and dark mode
Go to Settings
View Your Dashboard

See your financial overview at a glance.

  • Net Worth: Total assets minus liabilities
  • Account Summary: Balance across all accounts
  • Spending Charts: Visualize where your money goes
  • Recent Transactions: Latest activity in your accounts
View Dashboard

The Gemini API key enables the Receipt OCR (Optical Character Recognition) feature. It allows the app to:

  • Automatically extract text from receipt photos
  • Identify transaction amounts and merchants
  • Create transactions from receipt images

You can configure your own API key in Settings > API Configuration.

Regex patterns help extract specific information from receipt text. They're used to:

  • Extract dates in various formats
  • Find transaction amounts with currency symbols
  • Identify merchant names
  • Extract line items from receipts

Create and test patterns in Settings > Regex Patterns.
